Reachout

Reachout bridges the gap in AI accessibility by bringing helpful, everyday assistance to simple text messages - or voice calls. No smartphone required. No app download. No Wi‑Fi needed. Just send an SMS or dial the number, pick from simple menu options, and get clear, friendly answers on any phone - including feature phones used by seniors and anyone who wants tech that feels easy.

Multiple ways to connect

Reach Reachout via SMS or voice call - whatever fits the situation. No typing required for voice, no minutes needed for SMS. Choose what works best.

Simple menu-driven interaction

Whether texting or calling, users pick from simple numbered options (1 for weather, 2 for news, 3 to ask, 4 for help) and get friendly, concise answers.

Built for basic phones

No internet, no app, no data plan needed. Works on feature phones, rotary lines, and every device in between. Pure voice and text.

Built for DawsHacks mini hackathon by a team of four: Artem Brandt, Talon Dunbar, Yan Chi Ng, and Jaden Ho.
