Reachout

Reachout bridges the gap in AI accessibility by bringing helpful, everyday assistance to simple text messages. No smartphone required. No app download. No Wi‑Fi needed. Just send an SMS and get clear, friendly answers on any phone, including feature phones used by seniors and anyone who wants tech that feels easy.

Accessible by design

Reachout is built so that people who are less comfortable with apps and browsers can still access AI with a familiar text message.

Fast, familiar interaction

Users send a short SMS, pick from simple menu options, and get concise responses back — making the experience easy to understand and easy to use.

Helpful for real life

Weather, guidance, and general questions are all delivered in a format that works on feature phones and low-bandwidth connections.
